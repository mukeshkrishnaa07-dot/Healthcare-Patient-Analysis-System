# 🏥 Healthcare Patient Analysis System

## 📌 Overview

The **Healthcare Patient Analysis System** is a data analysis project that uses synthetic patient data to study health conditions and identify patient risk levels.

Since real healthcare data is often unavailable due to privacy and security concerns, this project generates a synthetic dataset containing **10,000 patient records** with important health attributes such as:

* Age
* Gender
* Blood Pressure
* Sugar Level
* Cholesterol
* Heart Rate

The project applies **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** techniques to understand patterns, distributions, and relationships among health parameters. Patients are classified into **Low Risk**, **Medium Risk**, and **High Risk** categories based on predefined medical thresholds.

Various visualizations such as bar charts, histograms, scatter plots, box plots, and heatmaps are used to present insights effectively.

# 🎯 Objectives

The main objective of this project is to perform **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on a synthetic healthcare dataset to understand patient health patterns and identify risk levels.

## Specific Objectives

1. Generate a synthetic dataset of 10,000 patients with key health parameters.
2. Perform data cleaning and preprocessing to ensure data quality.
3. Analyze data using statistical measures such as:

   * Mean
   * Median
   * Standard Deviation
4. Study important health metrics including:

   * Blood Pressure
   * Sugar Level
   * Cholesterol
   * Heart Rate
5. Classify patients into:

   * Low Risk
   * Medium Risk
   * High Risk
6. Identify high-risk patients who may require medical attention.
7. Perform group-based analysis:

   * Age-wise
   * Gender-wise
   * Risk-wise
8. Examine relationships between variables (e.g., Age vs Blood Pressure).
9. Visualize healthcare data using multiple chart types.
10. Derive meaningful insights to support healthcare decision-making.

# 📋 Results

The Healthcare Patient Analysis System was successfully implemented using Python and synthetic healthcare data.

## Key Findings

* Most patients fall into the Low and Medium Risk categories.
* A smaller percentage of patients were identified as High Risk.
* Blood Pressure, Sugar Level, and Cholesterol significantly influence patient risk classification.
* Older age groups generally showed higher health risk levels.
* High-risk patients often had elevated blood pressure and sugar levels.
* Correlation analysis revealed meaningful relationships among health indicators.
* Visualizations helped uncover trends, patterns, and outliers within the dataset.
